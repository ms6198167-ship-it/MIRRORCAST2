# Required iOS Info.plist keys

Add these to the iOS app target's generated Info.plist in Xcode (do not replace the complete generated plist with this note):

- `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription` (String): `MirrorCast sends your screen and selected audio to a receiver on your local network.`
- `NSBonjourServices` (Array of String): `_mirrorcast._tcp`

The Broadcast Upload Extension's Info.plist and extension point identifier should be generated by Xcode's Broadcast Upload Extension template. Use valid bundle identifiers and signing settings for both targets.
